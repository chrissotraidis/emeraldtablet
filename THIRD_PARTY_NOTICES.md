# Third-party notices

Emerald Tablet is a thin Apple wrapper around the pinned open-source
[Akhenaten](https://github.com/dalerank/Akhenaten) engine (AGPL-3.0). The
engine and the dependencies it fetches at build time remain under their
upstream licenses; the wrapper adds no vendored libraries of its own.

The exact versions and artifact hashes for each release candidate are recorded
in `build/release/SOURCE-MANIFEST.txt` (generated by
`scripts/generate-source-manifest.sh`) — the engine pin, the ordered patch
queue with per-patch SHA-256, and each packaged artifact's SHA-256.

License texts for the engine and its dependencies ship with the upstream
source; the corresponding-source link for the AGPL-modified engine is the
pinned engine repository plus this wrapper's patch queue
(`patches/akhenaten/`). See [RIGHTS_AND_LICENSES.md](RIGHTS_AND_LICENSES.md)
and the AGPL text in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
